# RISKS — open dangers and their mitigations

Severity: 🔴 critical · 🟠 high · 🟡 medium · 🟢 low
Status: `open` · `mitigated` · `accepted` · `closed`

| R-001 | **Financial dispute from a bug.** A wrong balance in a money system destroys trust permanently, and the community's whole reason for adopting this is trust. | 🔴 | open | Append-only ledger, invariant tests as CP gates, dual independent computation of every total, immutable monthly statements, full audit log. | agent |
| R-002 | **Unit-level arrears are visible to neighbours.** Socially significant in a small community; could cause real friction or embarrassment. | 🔴 | open | Board must approve this in writing before launch (ADR-005). | R-015 | **Separation of duties is theatre if one person holds every role.** | 🟡 | open | At least two distinct people as `admin`; expenses above a threshold require a countersign; the audit log is readable by the whole board. | owner/board |

| R-018 | **Authorization bug leaks private data, with no RLS backstop.** Application checks fail *open* if one is forgotten; RLS fails *closed*. | 🔴 | open | One SQL location enforced by lint; identity as a required argument (missing = compile error); ownership predicates inside the query strings; HTTP-level access tests as each role as the CP-1 gate, each watched failing first. | R-019 | **A quota is silently exhausted and residents discover it as an outage.** | 🟡 | open | `/admin/health` quota dashboard built in CP-3, thresholds at 50/70/85/95%; degradation order = block large uploads → block writes → **always preserve read access**; UptimeRobot alerts. | agent |

| R-021 | **Phone number treated as an identity key.** One number change, ownership transfer, or duplicate collision corrupts the link between a person, their unit, and their posted money — unrecoverable after real transactions exist. | 🔴 | mitigated | ADR-012: immutable internal id; `phone_identifiers` table with history, status, and a `replaced_by` chain; nothing foreign-keys to a phone number. | agent |

| R-023 | **Device loss becomes the new lockout mode.** Passkeys live on the device, so a lost or replaced phone locks the resident out. | 🟠 | open | Encourage a second passkey on another trusted device; issue single-use printed recovery codes at activation; assisted recovery requires **two** admins so one operator can never re-enroll alone. | agent + board |
| R-024 | **Maker–checker is theatre with one active admin.** A small volunteer board may have one person doing everything. | 🟠 | open | `/admin` displays a visible warning when only one active admin exists rather than pretending the control works; propose a `FINANCE_REVIEWER` read-and-review role that is cheap to staff. | owner/board |
| R-025 | **Delegate access outlives its purpose.** A son authorized once keeps access for years, or after a sale. | 🟡 | open | `delegate_authorizations` must carry `valid_to`; access ends automatically; a clock-advance test proves expiry; delegated actions are logged as "بالنيابة عن". | agent |
| R-026 | **Photos leak private information.** Album and receipt photos can capture children, flat interiors, documents, license plates, and GPS coordinates in EXIF. | 🟡 | open | Strip EXIF (especially geolocation) on upload; a publishing checklist before any album goes live; board consent policy for photographs of people. | agent + board |
| R-027 | **Egyptian PDPL (Law 151/2020) obligations unmet.** The system holds names, phone numbers, and financial records of hundreds of people. | 🟠 | open | Board formally named as data controller in writing; plain-Arabic privacy notice at first login; documented retention per data class; a qualified Egyptian lawyer reviews before production. | R-043 | **A correct ledger does not imply a correct report — and the report is what the board reads.** `v_unit_balance` compared dues from published fee periods against **every approved payment**, so a flat that paid its 5,000 ج.م الوديعة and none of its 6,000 ج.م subscription reported 1,000 ج.م outstanding instead of 6,000. `v_community_totals.receivables_piastres` reads that view, so the headline متأخرات on `/finance` was understated by the whole deposit pool — on 204 units, over a million pounds of arrears that never appeared. | R-044 | **`verify_demo.py` carried a comment asserting the wrong thing, and it passed for five sessions.** It read *"from_ledger includes deposits… from_payments includes them too… They must agree"* — documenting the R-043 defect as if it were the design. | R-045 | **`v_fund_balances` reported every fund as holding roughly nothing.** `LEFT JOIN accounts a ON a.id = pl.account_id AND a.type = 'asset'` reads like a filter and is not one — a LEFT JOIN's ON clause decides whether the right row attaches, never whether the left row survives. Income credits kept being netted against cash debits. The view is documented in `0005` as *"what makes الفلوس المتاحة للصرف honest"*; it was not honest, and `/finance` was spared only because `spendable` is computed from `v_account_balances` instead. **A view nothing reads is not harmless — it is a wrong answer waiting for its first caller.** | 🟠 | **fixed** | `migrations/0012_view_audit.sql` rebuilds it with the account-type restriction in a `WHERE` inside a correlated subquery, and adds `owed_piastres`. `tests/access/views.test.ts` asserts every fund against an independent recomputation. Two new demo invariants became possible only after this fix. | agent |
| R-046 | **`v_unit_ledger_balance` — the *independent second opinion* — had the same broken LEFT JOIN.** This view exists solely to be the second path that invariant 8 compares against ("two paths to one number is the oldest accounting control there is", its own comment). It escaped notice because `seed/demo/generate.py` happens to tag only income and liability lines with `unit_id`, never the cash line — an unwritten habit nothing enforces. `transparency.test.ts` tagged every line, the obvious reading of the column, and the view would have returned 0 for every unit. | 🔴 | **fixed** | Rebuilt in `0012`. **General lesson recorded in `INSIGHTS`: a control whose correctness depends on a data-entry convention is not a control.** `views.test.ts` uses a fixture that deliberately tags every line. | agent |

| R-048 | **`v_deposit_leakage` was narrower than its name, and contained dead code.** `JOIN payments p ON p.id = pl.entry_id OR …` compares a payment id to a journal-entry id — it can never match, and the `OR` also defeated the index. | R-056 | **Nothing forces a new account into a caption.** Both R-053 and R-054 were possible because "which accounts does this Arabic sentence cover?" existed only inside the sentence. Adding an account without deciding where it belongs now fails a test. **Only covers liability and fund accounts** — asset, income and expense accounts are still unguarded, because their captions are aggregates by `type` rather than by code. | agent |
| R-057 | **`newId()` produced 25-character ids for any prefix that is not exactly three letters.** It padded the body to a fixed 23, which is only right for `PAY`/`AUD`/`UOW` — the only prefixes it had ever been called with. `newId('JE')` and `newId('JL')` returned 25 characters and every `CHECK (length(id) = 26)` refused them. Exercised by expense posting, which generates `JE` and `JL` ids on every call. | agent |
| R-058 | **An operator could post an expense to the ledger.** `postExpense` was guarded by `expense.record` — which the `operator` role holds, because typing in the plumber's receipt is the whole point of that role. But **posting is approving**: it stamps `approved_by` on the journal entry and moves the treasury balance on every resident's screen. `03_RBAC §2` says an operator can never approve money, and C8 makes it absolute. | 🔴 | **fixed** | Guarded by `expense.countersign`, which the operator does not hold. Found because a test written to assert an operator is refused **watched one succeed** — the test was right and the code was wrong, which is the only reason that test was worth writing. | agent |
| R-059 | **Nothing posted expenses to the ledger at all.** `recordExpense` and `countersignExpense` captured that money was spent; neither moved the books. A recorded expense sat with `journal_entry_id IS NULL`, so `/finance` showed every piastre received and **none** of what was spent — the treasury overstated by the year's entire costs, invisibly. R-043's mirror image. | 🔴 | **fixed** | `lib/db/expenses.ts` posts Dr expense / Cr asset in one `db.batch()` with the status update and the audit row. `v_unposted_expenses` + a warning banner on `/finance` name the remaining gap in ج.م, so "spent but not in the books" is a number on a screen rather than a silence. | agent |
| R-060 | **Spending residents' deposits was only *detected*, not prevented.** R-049 added a banner. A banner is what you build when prevention is impossible — and here it was not impossible: an أمانة is not the village's money, so no expense may be drawn against a fund with `is_spendable = 0`. | 🟠 | **fixed** | `trg_expense_fund_spendable_ins/upd` refuse the write outright, asserted with every `lib/db/` guard bypassed. Reserve funds are covered by the same rule. **R-049's banner stays** — the trigger protects the path through `expenses`, and a hand-written journal entry could still do it. Prevention where possible, detection everywhere else. | agent |
| R-061 | **A wrongly-approved receipt was permanent.** `payment.reverse` had been in the capability matrix since CP-1 with nothing implementing it. Mirrors the **original** entry's lines rather than an assumed shape, so an overpayment's owner credit is withdrawn with the income instead of being left as a balance the village still owes. Arrears reappear by construction. | agent |
| R-062 | **⭐ The admin who approves a receipt could have quietly un-approved it.** Ordinary maker–checker ("the maker cannot approve") does not cover reversal. Without the mirror rule, one admin approves a family's receipt, takes the credit for it in person, and reverses it a month later with nobody else ever looking — the actual shape of small-community embezzlement. | 🔴 | **fixed** | `trg_payment_reversal_needs_second_admin` in the schema (ADR-024), plus `reversePayment` requiring a **named** `secondAdminId` on the record — reusing the pattern `changePhoneNumber` already uses for staff changes. Both names are stored, so "the treasurer said it was fine" is a row, not a memory. | agent |
| R-063 | **A reversal the resident is never told about is indistinguishable from theft.** Money disappears from a statement after they were told «اتقبل إيصالك ✅». In a compound built on suspicion, one family experiencing that undoes the project. | 🔴 | mitigated | The notification is written in the **same `db.batch()`** as the reversal, so "notify later" is not a representable state. `v_reversed_payments.notified` surfaces any gap. **Weakness recorded:** `notified` matches on kind + link_path, not payment id (`notifications` has no FK to a payment), so two reversals for one resident count as notified when only one message exists. A weak check that reads like a strong one. | agent |
| R-064 | **Migration `0015` recreates `notifications`, which is destructive.** SQLite cannot ALTER a CHECK constraint and the `kind` list needed `payment_reversed`. | 🟠 | accepted | **Safe only because this project has never been deployed**, so the table is empty everywhere it exists. Stated in bold inside the migration: if that changes before launch it must be rewritten to copy rows. Reusing `account_changed` was rejected — a resident filtering their notifications would never find it, and **a kind that lies is worse than a migration that is honest about being destructive**. | agent |
| R-065 | **Every ordinary decision was silent.** Approved, rejected, needs-info and duplicate sent the resident **nothing**; a reversal was the only event the system ever announced. So the everyday cases said nothing and the one rare bad case spoke — and a resident whose receipt sat at «تحت المراجعة» with no follow-up asks in the WhatsApp group, **which is the exact habit this product exists to replace**. `01_PRD` A4 asks for the approval notification by name. | 🔴 | **fixed** | `notifyDecision()` writes the message in the **same `db.batch()`** as the status change, so "decided but silent" is not a representable state. `v_payment_notifications` counts any that slipped through, and a test asserts it is empty. Every message names what to do next; a rejection ends «الرفض ده مش نهائي», and a **duplicate is not announced as a rejection** — the money arrived, it was counted once, and saying so prevents the phone call. | agent |
| R-066 | **A double-tapped approve would have sent the resident the same message twice.** `reviewPayment` is idempotent — the second call changes 0 rows — but a notification in the same batch is written regardless, on exactly the connection quality that makes people tap twice. | 🟠 | **fixed** | `notifications.payment_id` + partial unique index `ux_notif_payment_kind`, with `INSERT OR IGNORE`. Suppression at the **database**, not a check-then-insert in application code — two statements that must agree are two statements that will eventually disagree. A receipt approved and later reversed still gets two messages (different kinds), which is the correct reading of "told once". | agent |
| R-067 | **R-063's `notified` check was weaker than it looked, and is now closed.** It matched on `kind` + `link_path` because `notifications` had no link to a payment, so two reversals for one resident counted as notified when only one message existed. | 🟠 | **fixed** | `notifications.payment_id` (migration 0016). `v_payment_notifications` joins on the id and covers **every** terminal decision, not only reversal — a rejected receipt nobody was told about is the same failure in different clothes. | agent |
| R-068 | **A wrong Web Push key derivation fails SILENTLY.** RFC 8291 encryption that is subtly wrong produces a well-formed body the push service accepts with `201 Created` and the browser cannot decrypt. Every message would vanish with a success response and nothing on our side would look wrong — and push endpoints are unreachable from the build sandbox, so no live send can be tested. | 🔴 | mitigated | `tests/unit/webpush.test.ts` reproduces **RFC 8291 §5's published test vector byte for byte** — both keypairs, the auth secret, the salt and the exact expected body. That pins every intermediate value, which is stronger than "it seemed to arrive". **Delivery to a real phone is still unproven** and belongs to CP-7 alongside the D1 gate. | agent |
| R-069 | **A VAPID key change silently kills every subscription.** The public key is baked into each browser subscription; rotating it means every resident stops receiving notifications and must re-enable by hand — the same failure shape as changing the passkey RP ID (R-022), and just as invisible. | 🟠 | open | Documented in `generateVapidKeys()` and in Q23's answer. Generate **once**, store as a Worker secret, write it down. There is no automated guard yet because nothing can compare a deployed key to a stored one from here. | owner |
| R-070 | **Browser push does not reach everyone, and the board will assume it does.** It requires permission, and on iOS the site must be added to the home screen *before* Safari will even offer the prompt. Some proportion of residents will never do it. A board that believes «كله اتبلّغ» stops following up by hand — which is worse than knowing nobody was told. | 🟠 | mitigated | `v_push_reach` + `pushReach()` report residents / reachable / dead as a number the board can see. A test asserts the gap is visible rather than assumed. The inbox remains the record: **every message is readable at `/notifications` whatever the phone does**. | agent + owner |
| R-071 | **A push payload renders on a locked screen.** Anyone standing near the phone reads it. The amount, the receipt reference and the full reason stay behind the session. | agent |
| R-072 | **Money went out with no evidence attached.** `expenses.invoice_storage_key` existed from CP-1 and nothing ever wrote it, so a 12,000 ج.م maintenance line had **no proof** — the first thing a resident asks about, and precisely the suspicion this project exists to answer. | 🟠 | **fixed** | The invoice photo is a **second, optional step** after recording (`/admin/expenses/:id/invoice`), kept off the entry form so the form stays five fields with no JavaScript. `v_expenses_without_evidence` reports how much posted spending still has none, so the board knows the number before the general assembly asks for it. | agent |
| R-073 | **An invoice photo publishes a SUPPLIER's phone number and bank details to 204 residents.** Third-party personal data, from someone who never consented, under PDPL 151/2020 — and `01_PRD` C2's transparency requirement does not dissolve it. | 🟠 | mitigated | ADR-026, three layers: (1) the upload screen tells the operator **before the camera opens** that every resident will see it and to cover the number — the data never enters the system, which beats every later control; (2) visible to members **by default**, because a hidden-by-default photo silently undoes the feature; (3) `expense_invoices_public` lets the board close it **without a deploy** if a supplier objects. Automatic redaction was rejected: unreliable Arabic text detection that misses once is worse than none, because everyone stops looking. **The board has not been told this exists** — it belongs in the CP-8 manual and is not there yet. | agent + board |
